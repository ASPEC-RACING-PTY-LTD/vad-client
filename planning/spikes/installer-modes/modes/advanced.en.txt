FULL ACCESS TO ALL TOOLS (ADVANCED)

What it does
- Everything in Simple Folder Backups, plus:
- One-way and two-way sync of folders with your VaultDock server.
- Automations (rules that run backups, syncs or clean-ups for you).
- Discovery of installed apps, games and developer projects.
- Reinstalling apps on a new or repaired PC (with your confirmation each time).
- Backup of app settings and selected registry keys.
- Snapshots (Windows shadow copies) so files that are open and locked can
  still be backed up consistently.
- Protecting other Windows profiles on this PC, only when those users agree.
- Extra management screens.

Scope (what it touches)
- The folders, apps and settings you select.
- Other Windows profiles only after that user signs in, opens VaultDock and
  gives consent. VaultDock never stores Windows passwords.
- Windows system folders and system registry areas are always off limits,
  in both modes.

Permissions
- Installing needs administrator rights, as in Simple mode.
- The background service additionally gets the Windows backup and restore
  privileges, which are needed to create shadow copies of locked files.
- Installing apps uses winget, Chocolatey or Scoop and always asks you first.
- Two-way sync can change and delete files in the synced folders on this PC
  (deleted files go to the Recycle Bin).

Resources
- Download: after installation, VaultDock downloads extra components
  (snapshots, discovery and reinstall) from the official VaultDock releases
  on GitHub and checks each one before use. This adds roughly tens of MB.
- More CPU and disk activity during discovery scans and snapshots.
- Shadow copies use some temporary space on the drive being backed up.

Compared with Simple Folder Backups
- Simple only backs up and restores folders in your own profile.
- Advanced adds sync, automations, app and settings tools, snapshots and
  multi-profile protection, and needs more permissions and resources.

Changing your mind
- You can switch back to Simple later in Settings > Mode. Nothing is deleted:
  sync and app jobs are paused, and all backups stay browsable and
  restorable.
