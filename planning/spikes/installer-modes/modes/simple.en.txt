SIMPLE FOLDER BACKUPS

What it does
- You choose folders, for example Documents, Desktop or Pictures.
- VaultDock backs them up automatically on a schedule to your VaultDock server.
- You can browse your backed-up files and restore any file or folder,
  including older versions.

Scope (what it touches)
- Only the folders you pick, inside your own Windows profile.
- Files are read as you, with your normal Windows permissions. VaultDock
  cannot read files that you yourself could not open.
- Other people's profiles on this PC are never included.
- Windows system folders and program folders are always off limits.

Permissions
- Installing needs administrator rights once, because VaultDock adds a small
  background service that keeps backups running when the window is closed.
- The service gets only the few Windows privileges it needs to act on your
  behalf. It does not get "backup operator" rights, so it cannot read
  locked or protected system files.
- Files are encrypted on this PC before they are uploaded.

Resources
- Download and disk: only the core app (no extra components).
- Background use: low CPU and disk activity while a backup runs; idle
  otherwise.
- Some free disk space for a temporary upload queue.
- Network: uploads go only to the VaultDock server you connect to.

Compared with Full Access (Advanced)
- Simple has no sync, no automations, no app or game discovery, no app
  reinstall, no registry/settings backup, no snapshots of locked files and no
  protection of other Windows profiles.
- Files that are open and locked by another program (for example an open
  Outlook data file) may be skipped and retried later.

Changing your mind
- You can switch to Full Access later in Settings > Mode. You do not need to
  reinstall, and your backups and settings are kept.
